Areas of Interest

My interests reflect both my professional journey and my personal passions. They shape my career choices, academic pursuits, and the way I connect with my community.

Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

I am passionate about ICT innovation and its role in transforming institutions and communities. My interests include web application development, system administration, and computer networks. I enjoy exploring new technologies and applying them to real-world challenges.

Security and Incident Response

As a Police Officer, I have a strong interest in security operations and incident response. I am committed to understanding how technology can enhance public safety, streamline investigations, and improve institutional resilience against emerging threats.

Community Impact Projects

I believe technology should serve people. My interest in community-driven projects has led me to design solutions such as a waste management reporting application and a patient management system for healthcare institutions. These projects reflect my dedication to using ICT for social good.

Academic Research and Continuous Learning

I value education and continuous learning. My interests extend to academic research in computer networks, data communication, and emerging technologies. I enjoy studying advanced ICT concepts and applying them to practical scenarios that benefit institutions and communities.

Personal Development

Beyond my professional life, I am interested in personal growth, leadership, and mentorship. I enjoy sharing knowledge with peers, guiding younger professionals, and contributing to discussions that inspire innovation and responsibility in society.

Sports and Recreation

In my free time, I enjoy watching football, which keeps me connected to teamwork, discipline, and strategy—values that resonate with my professional life. I also play Mweso, a traditional indoor board game that requires deep concentration, patience, and tactical thinking.
